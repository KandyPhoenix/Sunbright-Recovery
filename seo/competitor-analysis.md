## Initial Positioning Insights

Based on quick competitor review:

1. **Most California rehabs do NOT accept Medi-Cal.** This is a massive underserved market and our biggest SEO + conversion differentiator.

2. **Most faith-based rehabs are heavy-handed about it.** Sunbright's positioning ("faith-integrated, never required") is genuinely rare and broadens appeal.

3. **Northern California is less saturated than Southern California.** Fewer competitors for `rehab northern california`, `rehab redding`, `rehab chico` queries.

4. **Premium rehabs lean on luxury photos and amenities.** We won't beat them on photography — but we can beat them on warmth, accessibility, and real human connection. Lead with Linda and the team, not the building.

5. **Co-occurring disorders is a high-intent query that few rehabs handle well.** Our connection to PHW (existing mental health practice) gives genuine credibility here.
