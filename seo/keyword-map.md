# Sunbright Recovery Center — Keyword Map

> Statewide California SEO targeting. Each page has one primary keyword and 2–3 secondary keywords.
> National rehab brands (American Addiction Centers, Hazelden, Caron, BetterHelp) own Tier 1.

## Content Frequency Targets

- **At launch:** 12 core pages
- **Month 1–3:** Add 4–6 blog posts (Tier 5 informational queries)
- **Month 3–6:** Add 6–10 more blog posts; expand FAQ on top-converting pages
- **Month 6–12:** Add city-specific landing pages if data shows demand (e.g., `/locations/redding`, `/locations/chico`)
- **Year 2:** Specialty pages (women's track, veterans, professionals) if Sunbright expands services

---

## Tracking & Reporting

- **Tool:** Google Search Console (primary), Google Analytics 4
- **Monthly review:**
  - Top 20 ranking keywords (movement up/down)
  - Top 10 entry pages
  - Top 10 queries showing impressions but no clicks (CTR optimization opportunities)
  - Conversion: insurance verification form submissions, phone clicks
- **Quarterly competitor check:** Search top 10 Tier 2/3 keywords, log who outranks us, identify content gaps
