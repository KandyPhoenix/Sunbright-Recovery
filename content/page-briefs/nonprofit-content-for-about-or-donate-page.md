## Three pillars

### Real care, not revenue targets
No upselling, no surprise charges, no quarterly earnings calls. Our only goal is your recovery.

### Donations are tax-deductible
Every contribution helps someone heal — and qualifies as a tax-deductible gift to a registered 501(c)(3).

### Community-accountable
Governed by a board, answerable to the people we serve — not Wall Street, not private equity.

## Additional supporting language for /about

Most rehabs in California are owned by national chains charging $30,000+ a month to maximize shareholder returns. Sunbright is different. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, every dollar — from program fees, donations, and grants — goes back into care. Not profits. Not investors. Care.
