# Sunbright Recovery Center — Brand Voice Guide

## Voice Foundation (Inherited from PHW Care)

Sunbright operates under the Parish Health and Wellness umbrella. Voice continuity with PHW reinforces trust, especially for clients/families who already know PHW for mental health services.

### Core voice attributes
- **Compassionate** — every reader is in pain or has someone they love in pain
- **Judgment-free** — never shame addiction, behavior, history, or the family
- **Hope-forward** — recovery is possible; lead with possibility, not severity stats
- **Evidence-based** — cite real therapies (CBT, MI, trauma-informed, family systems, relapse prevention)
- **Faith-integrated, never preachy** — faith available for those who want it, never required, never the lead unless on the "Our Approach" page
- **Family-inclusive** — addiction affects whole families; speak to spouses, parents, adult children
- **Direct about access** — Medi-Cal, sliding scale, insurance, affordability mentioned plainly

### What PHW does well that we keep
- Personal, warm tone — feels like a small caring practice, not a corporate chain
- Practical info upfront (insurance, what to expect, who you'll see)
- Clear practitioner credibility (years of experience, licenses)
- Real CTAs (phone number, not just contact forms)

## Sunbright-Specific Voice Adjustments

Rehab is different from outpatient mental health. People landing on a rehab site are often:
- In crisis or near-crisis
- A family member trying to save someone
- Comparing 5–10 sites in one sitting
- Skeptical of "treatment-mill" feel
- Worried about cost, judgment, privacy, and whether it will work

These adjustments address that.

### 1. Urgency-aware
Every page needs a clear, persistent next step. Don't bury CTAs.
- Sticky phone button on every page: **"Call 530-528-2342"**
- Secondary CTA on most pages: **"Verify Your Insurance"** or **"Schedule a Tour"**
- Above-the-fold CTA on Homepage, Programs, Admissions
- Avoid generic "Contact Us" — use action verbs ("Start Admissions", "Talk to Linda Today")

### 2. Less clinical in headings, more human
| Don't (clinical) | Do (human) |
|---|---|
| "Substance Use Disorder Treatment" | "Addiction Recovery" |
| "Medically Assisted Detoxification" | (We don't offer detox — refer out) |
| "Co-occurring Disorder Management" | "When Addiction & Mental Health Overlap" |
| "Therapeutic Modalities" | "How We Help You Heal" |

Use clinical terms in body copy and schema (for SEO accuracy), but H1/H2 should be human.

### 3. Privacy-forward
On every form, contact page, and admissions page, explicitly state:
- "Your call is private."
- "We follow HIPAA confidentiality."
- "We never share your information."

This addresses the #1 unspoken fear: "Will my employer/family/community find out?"

### 4. Outcome-focused, not despair-focused
**Don't lead with:**
- "Addiction kills 100,000 Americans a year"
- "Most people relapse"
- "It's never been worse"

**Do lead with:**
- "People recover every day. We've watched it happen for 20 years."
- "Healing happens in safe places. We built one."
- "There's a way back. Let us walk it with you."

Statistics can appear in body copy for credibility, but never as the hook.

### 5. Bed availability awareness
Residential rehab buyers often urgently need to know "can I get in this week?"
- Surface admissions timing on Residential page
- Add a "Current Availability" indicator (if Linda is willing to maintain it)
- "Most admissions within 3–5 days of first call" type language

### 6. Faith integration: tone matters
PHW is faith-influenced but serves everyone. Sunbright should be the same.
- Don't open Homepage with scripture
- Don't make faith a barrier to entry
- Do offer faith-integrated track on `/our-approach` page for those who want it
- Do mention prayer/spiritual support is available, not required
- Use "faith-integrated" not "Christian" in primary marketing (broader appeal); use "Christian rehab" as a targeted SEO keyword on `/our-approach` specifically

## Three Voice Rules for Every Page

When writing or reviewing copy, check each page against:

1. **Open with empathy, not data.** First sentence should meet the reader where they are emotionally.
2. **Make the next step obvious.** Reader should know exactly what to do next (call, verify, tour, read).
3. **Never shame the visitor or their loved one.** No moral language about addiction. No "rock bottom" tropes unless they're explicitly hopeful.

## Word Bank

### Words we use
recovery · healing · hope · journey · path · support · understand · family · compassion · evidence-based · trauma-informed · personalized · confidential · private · trusted · experienced · home · welcome · ready · today · together

### Words we avoid
- **junkie, addict (as noun), alcoholic (as noun)** — use "person with addiction", "person in recovery"
- **clean** (as in "stay clean") — use "in recovery", "sober"
- **failed, failure** — use "relapsed", "struggled"
- **suffer, suffering** (as primary frame) — use "struggle" or "going through"
- **must, should, have to** — use invitations: "you can", "we'll help you"
- **rock bottom** (cliché)
- **war on drugs** language

### Clinical terms reserved for body copy + schema only
substance use disorder · co-occurring disorder · medication-assisted treatment · partial hospitalization · intensive outpatient · relapse prevention plan · biopsychosocial assessment

## Reviewer Checklist (before any page ships)

- [ ] H1 is human, not clinical
- [ ] Opening sentence has empathy
- [ ] Phone CTA visible above the fold
- [ ] Insurance/Medi-Cal mentioned if relevant page
- [ ] HIPAA/privacy noted if form on page
- [ ] No shaming language (see Word Bank)
- [ ] Reading level: 7th–9th grade (test with Hemingway app)
- [ ] At least one mention of Linda or the team for credibility
- [ ] Clear "what happens next" by end of page
